# Code of Conduct

Monido is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want taking part in it (issues, pull requests, Discussions, translations on Weblate, reviews) to be welcoming and safe for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their first.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Critique code, wording and ideas with reasons and, where you can, a better alternative.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change; the reasons are given, and the decision can be discussed.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's personal or financial data, and use fictional examples in reports.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@monido.dev**. Reports are read by the maintainer and handled privately; the reporter's identity is not shared without consent. Security vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a participant, depending on the severity and whether the behaviour repeats. This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
